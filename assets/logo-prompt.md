# A-Share Monitor 环绕轨道图标

使用内置 image_gen 生图功能，参考用户提供的环绕轨道图标生成，并按用户要求去掉污渍与大块白点。最终采用干净的深蓝背景、浅色轨道细线与三个空心节点。`logo-source.png` 是选定的生成原图，`icon.png` 与 `app.ico` 由原图缩放、转格式导出。

## 最终生成提示词

Create a pristine flat graphic software logo inspired by the supplied reference icon. Square canvas, opaque. A perfectly clean solid dark blue-charcoal background, edge to edge, one even flat color with absolutely no shading, texture or mottling. At the center draw three elegant interlaced elliptical orbital loops in fine, crisp pale ivory outlines, with a few very small HOLLOW circular orbit nodes, like delicate line drawing. All three loops weave around the same empty central point. Three overlapping orbital paths only, balanced and spacious. The mark fills about 65 percent of the canvas width and height. Minimal precise vector logo aesthetic. NO large filled white circles, NO white stains or blotches, NO random specks, NO gradients, NO shadows, NO glow, NO transparency, NO distress, NO grunge, NO decorative dots, NO text, NO symbols in the center, NO chart. The dark background must look completely immaculate and uniform, and the ivory orbital lines must look clean and controlled. This is final small Windows app icon artwork, not a mockup.
